# AI-Generated Measures of Psychological Safety

This repository supports a study of how much an established organizational finding depends on the measure used to capture it. AI-generated psychological safety scales are treated as a sample of plausible measures, and the relationship between psychological safety and team learning behavior is estimated across them.

- [`multiscale-design/`](multiscale-design/): the multi-scale design, with its simulation, estimation, and power analysis
- [`pilot/`](pilot/): pilot item generation and synthetic factor analyses
