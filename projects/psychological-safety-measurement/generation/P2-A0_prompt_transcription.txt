You are developing candidate survey items for an academic research study.



Generate exactly seven survey statements measuring psychological safety in
work teams.


Requirements:

- Respondents are employed adults reporting their perceptions of a current
work team.

- Use "this team" as the referent throughout.

- Each statement must be answerable on a seven-point accuracy scale (1 =
very inaccurate; 7 = very accurate).

- Include four positively keyed and three negatively keyed items.

- Each item must express one clear idea.

- Avoid ambiguous wording, jargon, and double-barreled statements.

- Do not provide explanations, citations, or commentary.



The target construct is psychological safety.



Construct definition and boundaries:



Psychological safety refers to the belief that a team is safe for interpersonal
risk-taking. It concerns whether members anticipate embarrassment,
rejection, punishment, or interpersonal devaluation when taking risks such
as acknowledging mistakes, expressing uncertainty, asking for help, or
questioning decisions.



Psychological safety should be distinguished from:



1. Team efficacy: Confidence in the team's collective ability to accomplish
tasks successfully.


2. General positive team evaluation: Broad liking, satisfaction, or favorable
feelings toward the team.

3. Team learning behavior: Actions such as seeking feedback, discussing
errors, reflecting on work processes, experimenting, or adapting practices.



The statements should assess perceptions of interpersonal safety rather than
team competence, general satisfaction, or how frequently learning behaviors
occur.



No previously published scale items are supplied as reference material.



Return only valid JSON containing an "items" array with exactly seven
objects. Each object must contain:

- "item_number": Integer from 1 to 7

- "text": The survey statement

- "direction": Either "positive" or "negative"



Do not include any text outside the JSON object.
